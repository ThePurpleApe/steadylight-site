SteadyLight Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

SteadyLight is made by Jacob Bailey ("we"). It is a tutoring app that runs entirely on your own computer. It has no accounts and no servers of its own, and we never receive any of your information: not your name, your settings, or anything you write. This page explains exactly what SteadyLight keeps on your computer and when it uses the internet.

What SteadyLight keeps on your computer

Your sessions. Each tutoring session is an ordinary file in Documents\SteadyLight\Conversations. Like any file, anyone with access to your computer account can open it. If your Documents folder is synced or backed up (Windows often syncs it to OneDrive), your session files go wherever that sync or backup goes. That is done by Windows or your backup service, not by SteadyLight.

Your settings. The name you'd like to be called, your country, your school level, and your chosen tutor and theme, in %APPDATA%\SteadyLight. Your country is used only to show the right crisis helpline.

The AI tutor. The tutor's model is downloaded once to %LOCALAPPDATA%\SteadyLight and runs on your computer. What you type is processed there and is never sent anywhere.

Technical logs. Two short logs in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SteadyLight: one about SteadyLight starting (app and Windows versions, whether the tutor could use the graphics card), and one from the program that runs the tutor (timings and errors). Neither log records what you type or what the tutor replies. They are never sent anywhere.

%APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA% are hidden app folders: to open one, paste it into the address bar of File Explorer. In the Microsoft Store version, Windows keeps the settings, tutor and logs in SteadyLight's own private app folder instead. Sessions are in Documents either way.

When SteadyLight connects to the internet

SteadyLight itself connects to the internet only to download the AI tutor: once, when you first set it up, or if you choose another tutor later. The download comes from Hugging Face, which, like any website, receives standard connection information such as your IP address, plus basic technical details of the request (such as the version of the downloader SteadyLight uses). It does not include your name, settings or sessions. Hugging Face's handling of this is covered by the Hugging Face Privacy Policy.

Links you choose to open, such as a crisis helpline's website, open in your own web browser. SteadyLight has no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

If you seem to be in distress

SteadyLight checks your messages on your computer for signs that you may be in real distress, and if so, shows the crisis helpline for your country. Nobody is notified and nothing is reported or sent. So that SteadyLight doesn't ask you again, a short note is saved in that session's file: when the check happened and how you answered. It doesn't say what concerned SteadyLight or repeat your message. Deleting the session deletes the note. Exported chats and Advocate summaries never include this note, and the Advocate summary also leaves out the message that set off the check. An exported chat contains the whole conversation as you wrote it.

The check is automatic and can miss things. It is not a substitute for talking to someone. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a crisis line.

Sharing is always your choice

You can export a session as text or save an "Advocate summary" as a PDF to share with an instructor. These files are created on your computer and only go where you send them. An exported chat includes everything in the conversation, so look it over before sending it.

Microsoft

If you buy SteadyLight from the Microsoft Store, the purchase, download and updates are handled by Microsoft. SteadyLight's window is drawn by Microsoft Edge WebView2, part of Windows, which sends diagnostic data to Microsoft according to your Windows diagnostic data settings. This is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Deleting your data

A session: delete it on SteadyLight's All sessions page, or delete its file from Documents\SteadyLight\Conversations. Deleting it in SteadyLight removes the file permanently; it does not go to the Recycle Bin.

Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes its settings, logs and downloaded tutors.

Uninstalling the version from SteadyLight's own installer removes the tutor program's log and asks whether to remove the downloaded tutors. Your settings and the startup log stay in %APPDATA%\SteadyLight and %LOCALAPPDATA%\SteadyLight until you delete those folders.

Either way, your sessions in Documents stay until you delete them.

Students

SteadyLight is made for high school and college students. Because we never receive any information from the app, we hold nothing about any student, of any age, to share or delete. Everything SteadyLight keeps is on the student's own computer, as described above.

State and Regional Privacy Rights (CCPA, GDPR, etc.)

Depending on where you reside, privacy laws may give you rights regarding access to or deletion of your personal data. Because SteadyLight stores all session data and settings exclusively on your local device, you exercise full, direct control over your data and may access or delete it at any time. We don’t collect, sell or share your data, and never use it for advertising. For purchase data held by Microsoft, contact them directly.

If SteadyLight changes what it keeps or sends, this page will be updated before the new version is released, with a new effective date.
